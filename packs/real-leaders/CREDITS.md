# Real leaders pack - credits

Generated by `tools/assets/leaders/credits.py` from `packs/real-leaders/leaders.json`.
The avatars in `packs/real-leaders/characters/` are likenesses of real people in an optional pack (website
edition only, never the app-store build). Each combines a Microsoft Rocketbox body and head (MIT)
with a face texture projected from a freely licensed photograph, adapted (warped onto the head
texture layout, colour-matched, retouched; the head mesh was reshaped to the photo's proportions;
hair, beards and clothes are painted or modelled for the game). The adapted face textures are
derivative works of the photos and carry the same licence terms as the photo they come from.
